## Reading rules
- `v` = OCCT BRepCheck valid, `s` = OP_SOLIDS (correct count, not always 1).
- CONSISTENT: cut+common=vol(A) AND fuse=vol(A)+vol(B)-common within tol.
- SELF-INCONSISTENT: OCCT's own three results violate the identities; the
  odd cell (majority vote) must NOT be used as ground truth — use the
  identity-derived value; gate that cell on the invariants instead.
- degenerate: |vol|<0.01 (empty/grazing) — detected, never named.
- OCCT-INVALID shape: OP_VALID=0 but real volume — the volume satisfies the
  identities (usable as volume truth) but OCCT's own result shape fails
  BRepCheck: do NOT gate our validity against OP_VALID on these cells.
- isometry check: every B_cfg volume must equal vol(chair1); violations below.

### Two measurement points — they disagree, and that matters
An independent FreeCAD/OCCT run measured the IN-MEMORY boolean results
and found free edges on 5 cells: z15 cut 1 naked (2 solids), x20 cut 3,
z30x20 cut 1, x13y29 common 1, x13y29 fuse 1 — plus BRepCheck INVALID on
z30 fuse, z30x20 fuse, z37 fuse. Re-probing that run's EXPORTED STEP
files (table above) shows NAKED 0 everywhere and VALID 1 on z30/z30x20
fuse. The STEP export/import round trip HEALS OCCT's own free edges and
some invalidity. Consequences:
- oracle topology counts must state which measurement point they came
  from; in-memory OCCT is dirtier than its STEP round trip;
- our kernel's results are compared AFTER the same round trip, so any
  naked edge we still show is a defect the importer could NOT heal —
  strictly worse than OCCT's in-memory blemishes;
- z37 fuse stays INVALID through the round trip: that one is a genuine
  oracle defect, not a transient in-memory artifact.
